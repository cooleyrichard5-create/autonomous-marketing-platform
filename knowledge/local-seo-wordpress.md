# Local SEO and WordPress

Starter principles only. Verify current Google policies and technical requirements.

- Keep business name, address/service area, phone, and website information consistent.
- Build useful service pages for real services and locations; avoid thin doorway pages.
- Add original project photos, clear service descriptions, trust evidence, and contact options.
- Ask real customers for honest reviews without incentives or review gating.
- Earn relevant local links and citations; avoid link schemes.
- Improve page speed, mobile usability, titles, headings, internal links, and indexability.
- A Google Business Profile is not a substitute for a useful website or strong service delivery.
