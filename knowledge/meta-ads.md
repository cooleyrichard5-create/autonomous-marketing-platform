# Meta Ads

Starter principles only. Verify current platform policies and interface details against official documentation.

- Match campaign objective to the actual business outcome.
- Use clear creative that shows the service, customer problem, and result.
- Keep lead forms short; ask only questions that materially improve lead quality.
- Respond to leads quickly and record contact, estimate, booking, and sale outcomes.
- Evaluate cost per qualified lead and cost per acquired customer, not CTR alone.
- Test creative concepts and offers systematically.
- Never claim an ad is live or changed unless the platform confirms it.
