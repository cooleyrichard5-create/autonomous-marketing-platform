# Marketing Foundations

Starter principles only. Replace or supplement these with vetted source material.

- Start with the customer's problem, buying trigger, and service area.
- Track qualified leads, booked estimates, close rate, average job value, gross profit, and customer acquisition cost.
- Separate awareness, consideration, and conversion campaigns.
- Use a clear offer, credible proof, and one primary call to action.
- Test one meaningful variable at a time when practical.
- Do not optimize for clicks when the business needs profitable customers.
- Confirm tracking before increasing ad spend.
