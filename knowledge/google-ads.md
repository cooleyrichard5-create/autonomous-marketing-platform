# Google Ads

Starter principles only. Verify current platform features and policies against official documentation.

- Separate high-intent service searches from research-oriented searches.
- Build tightly themed campaigns and ad groups around services and geography.
- Use negative keywords to reduce irrelevant traffic.
- Ensure landing pages match the search intent and provide a clear conversion path.
- Configure and test conversion tracking before scaling.
- Evaluate search terms, qualified leads, booked work, and profit.
- Set budget and bidding limits appropriate to business cash flow.
